About Anaemia AI

Pioneering explainable AI for anaemia prediction and clinical decision support

Project Overview

HemaScope XAI represents a breakthrough in medical AI, combining cutting-edge machine learning with explainable artificial intelligence to revolutionize anaemia detection and management.

Our system goes beyond traditional machine learning by focusing on actionability and transparency, providing healthcare professionals with not just predictions, but clear explanations and actionable insights.

Key Innovations
XAI Integration

First system to integrate both SHAP explainability and counterfactual analysis specifically for anaemia prediction, providing comprehensive decision support.

Risk Index

Introduction of Anaemia Risk Index as a composite biomarker that combines multiple parameters into a single, interpretable risk score.

Clinical Focus

Designed specifically for clinical decision support with explanations tailored to healthcare professionals' needs and workflows.

Privacy First

Local processing ensures patient data never leaves the healthcare facility, maintaining complete privacy and compliance.

Technical Architecture
Machine Learning Core

Support Vector Machine with optimized hyperparameters for maximum accuracy

Explainability Layer

SHAP and counterfactual algorithms for transparent decision-making

Clinical Interface

Intuitive web application designed for healthcare professionals
