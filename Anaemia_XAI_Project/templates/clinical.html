Research Use and Limitations

How to interpret prototype results and what to know before submitting data

Risk Interpretation Guidelines
Low Risk
Risk Index: 0-30

Lower range of the prototype's rule-based score. This score is not a probability.

Interpretation:
  • Check the entered CBC values
  • Use the report's reference ranges for context
  • A low score cannot rule out anaemia
Moderate Risk
Risk Index: 31-60

Middle range of the prototype's rule-based score.

Interpretation:
  • Check units and transcription
  • Consider the full report when interpreting values
  • The score does not set follow-up timing
High Risk
Risk Index: 61-100

Upper range of the prototype's rule-based score.

Interpretation:
  • The score does not measure severity
  • The score does not determine urgency
  • Clinical interpretation requires other information

Ethical Considerations
Patient Privacy

Manual inputs and uploaded PDFs are sent to the hosted Flask server for processing. Use de-identified reports.

Clinical Supervision

AI predictions should always be reviewed by qualified healthcare professionals. A prototype prediction does not establish a diagnosis.

Limitations
  • Predictions based on limited parameters
  • Cannot replace comprehensive clinical evaluation
  • May not account for rare conditions
  • Population-specific performance variations
Fixed Model

Predictions use a fixed PyTorch checkpoint. Submissions do not retrain it.

Data Privacy & Security
What the Server Receives
  • Manual Entry: Submitted form values
  • PDF Upload: Uploaded PDF contents
  • Gender: Form value used if the PDF does not specify it
  • Registration: Account details if you register
  • Before Uploading: Remove identifiers from the report
Application Handling
  • PDF text is extracted on the server
  • Prediction requests do not save an account record
  • Registration stores account details separately
  • Prediction diagnostics omit submitted clinical values
Privacy Limitations
  • Hosting retention practices have not been independently verified
  • Healthcare privacy compliance has not been established
  • Use synthetic values when exploring the prototype
  • Submit only data you are authorized to share
