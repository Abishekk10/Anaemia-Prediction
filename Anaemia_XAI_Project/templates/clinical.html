Clinical Decision Support

Risk interpretation, follow-up guidance, and ethical considerations

Risk Interpretation Guidelines
Low Risk
Risk Index: 0-30%

Patient parameters within normal ranges. Low probability of anaemia.

Recommendations:
  • Routine monitoring
  • Annual screening
  • Maintain healthy lifestyle
Moderate Risk
Risk Index: 31-60%

Borderline parameters detected. Further evaluation recommended.

Recommendations:
  • Comprehensive blood work
  • Nutritional assessment
  • 3-6 month follow-up
High Risk
Risk Index: 61-100%

Significant abnormalities detected. Immediate clinical attention advised.

Recommendations:
  • Urgent medical consultation
  • Specialist referral
  • Comprehensive diagnostic workup
Follow-up Protocol
Monitoring Schedule
Risk Level Follow-up Tests
Low 12 months CBC only
Moderate 3-6 months CBC + Iron studies
High 1-4 weeks Full panel
Recommended Laboratory Tests
  • Complete Blood Count (CBC) with differential
  • Iron Studies: Serum iron, ferritin, TIBC
  • Vitamin Levels: B12, folate, vitamin D
  • Reticulocyte Count: Bone marrow response
  • Peripheral Smear: Morphology assessment
  • Inflammatory Markers: CRP, ESR
Ethical Considerations
Patient Privacy

All patient data is processed locally and never transmitted to external servers. The system maintains strict confidentiality and complies with healthcare data protection standards.

Clinical Supervision

AI predictions should always be reviewed by qualified healthcare professionals. The system is designed to augment, not replace, clinical judgment.

Limitations
  • Predictions based on limited parameters
  • Cannot replace comprehensive clinical evaluation
  • May not account for rare conditions
  • Population-specific performance variations
Continuous Learning

The model is regularly updated with new clinical data to improve accuracy and reduce bias in predictions across diverse patient populations.

Data Privacy & Security
Privacy Commitment
  • No Data Storage: Patient information is not stored after analysis
  • Local Processing: All computations occur on the local system
  • No External Transmission: Data never leaves the healthcare facility
  • Anonymous Analytics: Only anonymized performance metrics are collected
  • HIPAA Compliance: System designed to meet healthcare privacy standards
Security Measures
  • End-to-end encryption for data in transit
  • Secure authentication protocols
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access logging and monitoring
Compliance Standards
  • Healthcare Data Protection Act compliance
  • Clinical laboratory improvement amendments

  • International data protection standards
