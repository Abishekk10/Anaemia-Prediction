Understanding Counterfactual Reasoning

Counterfactual analysis identifies the smallest changes needed in patient parameters to change the prediction outcome.

Instead of just telling you "this patient is anaemic," our system shows you exactly what needs to improve to reach a non-anaemic state. This provides actionable insights for treatment planning and patient management.

Clinical Value: Counterfactuals help clinicians understand which parameters are most critical for improving patient outcomes and prioritize interventions.

How Counterfactuals Are Generated
Optimization Algorithm

Our system uses gradient-based optimization to find the minimal changes needed to cross the decision boundary. The algorithm prioritizes changes that are:

  • Clinically realistic and achievable
  • Minimal in magnitude
  • Most impactful on prediction outcome
Clinical Constraints

The counterfactual generator respects medical constraints:

  • Normal physiological ranges
  • Gender-specific reference values
  • Realistic treatment timelines
  • Clinical feasibility of changes
Clinical Benefits of Counterfactual Analysis
