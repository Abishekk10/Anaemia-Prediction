Explainable AI with SHAP

Understanding how our AI model makes anaemia predictions

What is SHAP?

SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) is a game theory approach to explain the output of any machine learning model.

In our anaemia prediction system, SHAP helps us understand which blood parameters contribute most to each prediction, making the AI's decision-making process transparent and trustworthy for healthcare professionals.

Why Explainability Matters: In healthcare, AI decisions must be interpretable. SHAP provides clear, quantitative explanations for each prediction.

Educational Value

Regulatory Compliance

Meets healthcare AI requirements for explainability and transparency in decision-making.

Technical Implementation
SHAP Values Calculation

We use TreeExplainer for our SVM model to compute SHAP values efficiently. Each feature gets a contribution score that shows how it pushed the prediction away from the baseline average.

Integration with Predictions

SHAP explanations are generated in real-time for each prediction, providing immediate insights into the decision-making process without compromising prediction speed.

Note: SHAP explanations are designed to supplement, not replace, clinical judgment. Always consider patient history and clinical context.
