Evidence and Scope
Evaluation Method
  • Preprocessing: Exact duplicate rows removed, keeping the first occurrence
  • Split: Stratified 80:20 split with random_state=42
  • Artifacts: Saved checkpoint and scaler used without refitting
  • Classification: Sigmoid threshold of 0.5, with Result=1 as the positive class
Limits of the Evidence
  • Validation: Independent clinical validation has not been performed.
  • Documentation: Clinical trials and regulatory clearance are not documented.
  • Outcomes: The holdout did not measure clinical workflows or patient outcomes.
  • Use: Predictions do not establish a diagnosis.
